Snagd Up Dispute Process

Last updated: 19th November 2026

1About this Dispute Process

1.1
The purpose of this Dispute Process is to set out how a Buyer may lodge a dispute about a Product purchased through the APP, and how we decide whether the funds held by the Payment Processor for that sale are released to the Seller or returned to the Buyer.
1.2
Capitalised words have the meaning given in the Snagd Up User Licence Agreement (Licence). If there is any inconsistency between this Dispute Process and the Licence, the Licence prevails.
1.3
We are not a party to any agreement between a Buyer and a Seller. Our role in a dispute is limited to deciding whether the funds held by the Payment Processor are released to the Seller or returned to the Buyer, in whole or in part.
1.4
We make each decision acting reasonably, against the criteria set out in this Dispute Process.
1.5
A decision we make under this Dispute Process does not determine the legal rights of a Buyer or a Seller against each other, and does not prevent either of them from pursuing those rights directly against the other as set out in the Licence.

2What counts as a valid claim

2.1
A Buyer may lodge a dispute by email to hello@snagdup.com.au if:
(a)
a Product to be delivered by the Seller has not been received;
(b)
the Product was lost or damaged before it was delivered to the Buyer’s nominated delivery address by a courier or postal service arranged by the Seller;
(c)
the Product is faulty, damaged or missing parts, and this was not disclosed in the Product listing;
(d)
the Product does not correspond in all material respects with the description and photographs in the Product listing, including its stated condition, quantity or dimensions; or
(e)
the Product is one that the Licence prohibits from being listed or sold through the APP.
2.2
The following are not valid claims:
(a)
a change of mind;
(b)
a defect, damage or missing part that was disclosed in the Product listing;
(c)
loss of or damage to the Product after it is collected by the Buyer or delivered to the Buyer’s nominated delivery address;
(d)
for a Product that is collected, any issue raised after the Buyer has provided the OTP to the Seller, as providing the OTP confirms that the Buyer accepts the condition of the Product and agrees to the funds being released to the Seller; and
(e)
any dispute lodged after the funds have been released to the Seller.

3Timeframe to lodge a dispute

3.1
A Buyer must lodge any dispute about a Product before the funds are released to the Seller:
(a)
for a Product that is collected, by not providing the OTP to the Seller at handover; and
(b)
for a Product that is delivered, before the Buyer confirms receipt through the APP and within 3 Business Days after delivery of the Product is shown by the carrier’s tracking information or the Seller’s proof of delivery.
3.2
If tracking information or proof of delivery shows a Product as delivered but the Buyer has not received it, the Buyer must lodge the dispute within the time required under clause 3.1(b).
3.3
If delivery of a Product is not shown by the carrier’s tracking information or the Seller’s proof of delivery within 10 Business Days after Payment Confirmation, the Buyer may lodge a dispute on the basis that the Product has not been received.
3.4
If a Buyer does not lodge a dispute in time, the funds are released to the Seller under the Licence.

4Evidence

4.1
When lodging a dispute, the Buyer must provide:
(a)
a short description of the problem;
(b)
photographs of the Product as received, clearly showing the problem and, for a Product that is delivered, the packaging and delivery label; and
(c)
for a Product that has not been received, any tracking information the Buyer has.
4.2
We will also consider the Product listing and any messages exchanged between the Buyer and the Seller that are provided to us by email. For example, we may compare the Buyer’s photographs with the listing photographs and the condition stated in the Product listing.
4.3
We will give each of the Buyer and the Seller the information the other provides where (in our view) it is reasonably necessary to allow them to respond or to resolve the dispute.

5Seller response

5.1
We will notify the Seller by email within 2 Business Days after a dispute is lodged.
5.2
The Seller may respond by email within 3 Business Days after being notified by:
(a)
offering the Buyer a full refund without return of the Product;
(b)
offering the Buyer a partial refund, with the Buyer keeping the Product;
(c)
offering the Buyer a full refund on return of the Product; or
(d)
disputing the claim and providing their own evidence, such as photographs taken before dispatch or handover, and proof of postage or delivery.

6Buyer response

6.1
We will give the Seller’s response to the Buyer by email. The Buyer may reply to the Seller’s response and accept or reject any offer made by the Seller, by email within 3 Business Days after we give the Buyer the Seller’s response. An offer that the Buyer does not accept within that time lapses.

7Further information and agreed outcomes

7.1
We may ask either party for further information, which must be provided within 2 Business Days. If a Buyer or Seller does not respond within the time required for any step in this Dispute Process, we will decide the dispute on the information we have.
7.2
If the Buyer and the Seller agree an outcome at any stage and each of them confirms it to us by email, we will decide the dispute in accordance with that agreement.

8Outcome, returns and refunds

8.1
We will decide whether the funds held by the Payment Processor are:
(a)
released to the Seller in full;
(b)
returned to the Buyer in full; or
(c)
returned to the Buyer in part, with the balance released to the Seller.
8.2
In deciding a dispute:
(a)
we consider the Product listing, any messages between the Buyer and the Seller provided to us by email and the information the Buyer and the Seller provide under this Dispute Process;
(b)
we return funds to the Buyer only if we are satisfied that it is more likely than not that the Buyer has a valid claim under clause 2.1;
(c)
for a claim that a Product has not been received, we treat the Product as received only if the carrier’s tracking information or the Seller’s proof of delivery shows that it was delivered to the Buyer’s nominated delivery address; and
(d)
we return only part of the funds to the Buyer where the Buyer and the Seller agree to that outcome, or where the claim is that the quantity of the Product is less than the quantity stated in the Product listing, in which case the part returned is in proportion to the shortfall.
8.3
We will give both the Buyer and the Seller a short written outcome with reasons by email, within 5 Business Days after the last response or further information is due.
8.4
Where the Buyer is required to return the Product under the Licence, the Buyer must send the Product to the Seller within 5 Business Days after we give the Buyer the Seller’s return address by email, using a tracked service, and email the tracking number to us. The Product must be returned in the same condition in which it was received. If the Buyer does not send the Product within that time, or any longer time we allow on the Buyer’s reasonable request, the dispute will be closed and the funds released to the Seller. Where we decide that only part of the funds is to be returned to the Buyer, the Buyer keeps the Product, unless we decide otherwise. We will not require the Buyer to return a Product that the Licence prohibits from being listed or sold through the APP, or that cannot lawfully or safely be sent. The Seller is responsible for paying return postage. If the Seller does not do so, we may suspend the Seller’s access to the APP.
8.5
The refund is released through the Payment Processor to the Buyer’s Payment Method once the Seller confirms receipt of the returned Product to us by email, or tracking information shows it has been delivered to the Seller. The Payment Processor and the Buyer’s bank may take several Business Days to process the refund.
8.6
If the Buyer asks their card issuer or payment provider to reverse the payment for a Product while a dispute about that Product is open, we may close the dispute. The funds are then dealt with in accordance with the outcome of that request and the Licence.

9Internal review

9.1
If the Buyer or the Seller disagrees with our decision, they may request one internal review within 2 Business Days after receiving the outcome, by email to hello@snagdup.com.au or through the contact functionality in the APP. The request must explain why they disagree and include any new evidence.
9.2
Where practicable, the review will be conducted by a different member of our team from the person who made the original decision. We will give both the Buyer and the Seller the review outcome, with reasons, within 5 Business Days. Subject to clause 10, we do not act on a decision, and the funds continue to be held, until the time to request an internal review has passed or, if a review is requested, the review is complete. The review outcome is final for the purposes of the APP.

10Maximum holding period

10.1
Funds for a sale cannot be held for more than 90 days after Payment Confirmation (Holding Limit).
10.2
If a dispute is open 5 Business Days before the Holding Limit ends, we will notify the Buyer and the Seller by email and, before the Holding Limit ends:
(a)
if we have made a decision under clause 8.1, we will give effect to our most recent decision, except that where the Buyer is required to return the Product and has not sent it in accordance with clause 8.4, the funds will be released to the Seller; and
(b)
if we have not made a decision, the funds will be returned to the Buyer where the Product has not been collected, or delivery of the Product is not shown by the carrier’s tracking information or the Seller’s proof of delivery, and will otherwise be released to the Seller.
10.3
The dispute is closed when funds are returned or released under clause 10.2.

11Contact

11.1
You may contact us about a dispute by email to hello@snagdup.com.au or through the contact functionality in the APP.
